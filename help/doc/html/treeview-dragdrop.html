TreeView Drag/Drop

This example demonstrates how to update a TreeView by dragging and dropping branches.

  1. In the salary field, type a mathematical expression.
  2. Drag an employee from one department to another to perform the transfer. You can drop the employee on the new department or on any employee in the new department. As you drag, notice that the current drag target (the new department) is highlighted. When the employee is dropped, it is inserted in the proper place in the department based on alphabetical order.
  3. Click Update to save the changes, or Cancel to cancel them.
