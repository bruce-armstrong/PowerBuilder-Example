MAPI Mail - Receive Mail

Clicking Read Inbox reads the contents of your mail inbox into the listing. You can click on a message to see who it is from, whom it is sent to, the subject, and the message. In addition, the DW (DataWindow) flag indicates that this message contains a DataWindow sent from the Send Mail example. Clicking on the flag retrieves the message, rebuilds the DataWindow, and retrieves the data into it. The DataWindow attachment was created using a PSReport format and the SaveAs() function.

Deprecated

MAPI mail is deprecated, and the MAPI examples are planned for removal. MAPI needs a MAPI mail client such as classic Outlook for Windows, which Microsoft supports until at least 2029. The new Outlook for Windows does not provide MAPI. PowerBuilder has no built-in replacement for reading a mailbox.
