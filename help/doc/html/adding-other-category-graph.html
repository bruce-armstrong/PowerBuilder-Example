Adding an Other Category to a Graph

This example shows a graph of the Percentage of Inventory of each product with a quantity greater than 30. All other products (those with quantity less than 30) are lumped together in the Other category.

The number in the upper left corner of the graph is a computed column called other_quantity with the following expression:

If( quantity <= 30 , quantity , 0 )

The category labels are set as follows:

If(quantity <= 30, "Other", description)

With the following value:

If(quantity <= 30, sum(other_quantity), quantity)

This results in the Other category as shown.
