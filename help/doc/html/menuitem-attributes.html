MenuItem Attributes

Click Menu 1 or Menu 2 to change the current menu. The current menu is displayed as three sets of menu items, corresponding to the different levels of the current menu.

Click any level to highlight the menu and show its attributes. Notice that any menu items under selection are listed in the DataWindows on the right. Experiment with changing the attributes of the menu items.

Example

Click File in either menu. Its submenu items appear in the second DataWindow. Click the Exit menu item in the second DataWindow. Change the enabled, visible, and checked attributes, and use the window's menu to view the changes. To change the MicroHelp of the Exit menu item, enter the new MicroHelp and press tab or enter.

Experiment with changing the attributes of the Exit toolbar item.
