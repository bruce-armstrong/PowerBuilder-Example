Towers of Hanoi

The puzzle involves three pegs. The problem is to move all disks from the left peg to the right peg according to certain rules:

Rules

  • When you move a disk is moved, you must place it on one of the three pegs.
  • You can move only one disk at a time.
  • You can move a disk only from the top of the stack (you cannot move a disk that has another disk on top).
  • You cannot place a larger disk on top of a smaller one.

Instructions

  1. Select the number of disks to be used using the disk edit box. More disks requires more moves to solve problem.\
  2. Click Start to begin the process. Once it has begun, you must let the problem go to completion.
  3. Click Single Step to view each move. The moves will stop until you click a button to continue.

Legend of the Towers of Hanoi

The priests in the Temple of Bramah were given a puzzle consisting of a golden platform with three golden needles on which were placed 64 golden disks. The world was to end when they had successfully finished moving the disks, using the rules given above.
