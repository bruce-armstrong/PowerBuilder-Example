Graphs and Shared DataWindows

The three graphs across the bottom share data with the primary DataWindow at the top. Any changes are shown in the graphs immediately.

The pie chart and the bar chart exclude terminated employees from their graphs. Change the employee status to terminated in a few different departments. Notice how the average age and salary percentage changes. Also note the total count by employee status changes in the column graph.

Changing the employees salary and birth date also changes the graphs.
