Department Reports

Choose a department from the department listing, and then choose a report from the File menu. Any number of report instances can be opened. To view reports for a different department, select another department from the department listing and repeat the same technique.

You can also drag a department into an open report sheets. The report changes to that department.

Example

Choose the finance department in the department listing sheet. Open all three reports from the File menu. Tile the sheets by clicking Window > Tile. Now, drag department names from the department sheet into the open report sheets.
