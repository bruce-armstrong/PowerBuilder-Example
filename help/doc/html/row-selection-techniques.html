Row Selection Techniques

To select and highlight a row in the DataWindow, click the row.

To select and highlight a contiguous group of rows, click the first row, then press and hold the Shift key while clicking the last row in the group.

To select and highlight a group of multiple rows anywhere in the DataWindow, press and hold Control, then clicking the rows you want to select.

To unselect (and unhighlight) one or more rows in a group of selected rows, press and hold Control, then click the rows to unselect.
